    A group of shapes inside a ShapeGroupRun or ShapeCanvasRun, which moves, scales and rotates as one.

    Its children are positioned relative to each other, like the children of a ShapeGroupRun, and transformation.offset places the group's top-left corner. Connectors in the group can only join shapes in the group, but connectors outside it can attach to the shapes in it.
