    Type Alias IShapeConnectorOptions

    A connector between two shapes in a ShapeGroupRun or ShapeCanvasRun.

    The connector is drawn from one shape to the other, so it doesn't need a size or position. On a ShapeCanvasRun, Word keeps it attached when either shape is moved.

    When several connectors with arrowheads meet at the same point of a shape with straight sides, such as a rectangle, or several connectors join the same two shapes, their ends are spread out along the side so they don't overlap.
