    A stock chart (c:stockChart): each category's high and low prices joined by a line, with its closing price, and if given, its opening price and the volume traded, as Word's "High-Low-Close", "Open-High-Low-Close", "Volume-High-Low-Close" and "Volume-Open-High-Low-Close" charts. Each price list has one value for each category, in order, and null leaves a gap.
